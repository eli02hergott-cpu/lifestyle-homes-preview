About this home

MOVE IN READY JUST COMPLETED and includes both your Grand Harbor Membership and 1 year of family golf dues paid in advance for you! Start your new life with Lifestyle Home’s new McKenna Plan which delivers exceptional farmhouse open cottage design offering everything your lifestyle desires in a spacious first floor–with plenty of room for guests, offices, fitness rooms upstairs. The large foyer reveals the spectacularly vaulted ceiling that creates a light-filled, inviting Great Room with fireplace, and a wall full of glass doors that open to your covered porch with a spectacular view of the 10th hole at Grand Harbor Golf Course! Your family will cherish memories of gatherings in the spacious, airy dining room right off the foyer. Casual, open plan kitchen invites gatherings around a huge quartz island. You’ll love the cabinet space–plus a well planned corner pantry that will hold all that coveted cookware and supplies. You’ll delight in spending time in the charming, sunny dining/breakfast room, experiencing nature and watching the golfers. Relax in the spacious primary suite which has tray ceilings, views of the golf course and a sumptuous bath with soaking tub, large tiled and glass enclosed shower, dual sinks, and two generous walk in closet spaces. Your casual guests will appreciate the powder room just off the kitchen. And you’ll love the laundry room with it’s additional storage opportunities and a wet sink–great for cleaning those golf shoes or bathing that puppy. The open staircase leads to the guest and flex suites–two bedrooms, two bathrooms and a very generous finished bonus room/bedroom with access to great attic storage. Your family and friends will spend hours enjoying grilling and relaxing on your 28′ long covered back porch. Of course, you’ll experience luxurious flooring, granite and/or quartz countertops, modern fixtures, deep crown molding and options for wainscoting, judges paneling and ship lap accents, soft close cabinetry, vented microwave fan, gas heat, instant on hot water heater, insulation above code, beautifully landscaped and irrigated yard outside of your generous 2.5 car garage. Best of all, this home includes membership to the prestigious Grand Harbor Golf & Yacht Club in this spectacular gated community offering a grand lifestyle filled with golf, fitness, pool, marina, pickleball, tennis, playground, boat storage, clubs, events, restaurant and so much more. Grand Harbor is serenely situated on Lake Greenwood, but is close to both the historic town of Ninety Six SC and the bustling town of Greenwood SC with exceptional healthcare facilities and easy shopping.
