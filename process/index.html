Home/Building Process
Our Building Process

Let's build your dream home.

We're here to help every step of the way. Here's exactly what to expect, and roughly how long each stage takes.

Phase 1

Plan & design

  1. 01

    Your home and homesite

    A day to a few months

    Explore our collection to find the right plan, or modify one to fit the way your family lives. We'll help you find the homesite that brings it to life, and connect you with lenders we trust for fast, easy, low-rate financing.

  2. 02

    Design and finish selections

    A day to a few weeks

    Traditional, modern or farmhouse. Choose your exterior in brick, stone, Hardie plank or shake, then your interior: wainscoting and deep crown, shaker or shiplap, plus flooring, tile, lighting, plumbing fixtures, cabinetry and appliances. Rather not choose? Our team will happily do it for you.

Phase 2

Build

  1. 03

    Beginning construction

    About a month

    We work with your community's Architectural Review Board and manage all County permitting, so there are no surprises. Then we clear the homesite, stake the house and lay the foundation: crawl space, slab or basement.

  2. 04

    Framing, roof and windows

    About two months

    Your home comes out of the ground within weeks and is under roof about two months after the foundation. TechShield radiant-barrier roof sheathing and energy-efficient windows and doors keep summer heat out and winter warmth in.

  3. 05

    Inside the walls

    About a month

    Plumbing, HVAC and electrical rough-in, then insulation that goes beyond State requirements in walls, attic and ductwork. Every step is checked by our own quality control as well as County inspectors.

  4. 06

    Drywall, cabinetry and trim

    About three weeks

    This is when your house becomes a home. Drywall is hung and finished, cabinetry is installed, and the trim package you chose is carefully crafted.

  5. 07

    Finishing the exterior

    About a month

    Stone, brick, Hardie board, board-and-batten, cedar columns, vaulted porches, screened living, outdoor kitchens. Whatever you envisioned for your showplace comes to life.

Phase 3

Finish

  1. 08

    Paint and color

    About two weeks

    Walls, ceilings and trim in every room (garage included) are painted to your specifications, followed by exterior paint, fascia and soffit.

  2. 09

    Final interior finishes

    Three to five weeks

    Flooring and tile are installed by experienced craftsmen, along with plumbing fixtures, mirrors, hardware, shelving, interior and exterior lighting, and gutters.

  3. 10

    Driveway and landscaping

    About two weeks

    Sod, mulch, shrubs, trees and irrigation to suit your community, plus hardscape like fire pits and built-in outdoor kitchens. You can work directly with our landscapers on changes.

Phase 4

Move in

  1. 11

    Your walkthrough

    7–10 days before closing

    Walk the finished home with our team, review the construction and installations, and raise anything you want addressed. We deliver every home beautiful, well built and clean.

  2. 12

    Orientation and closing

    Closing day

    A team member spends a couple of hours showing you how everything in the home works, walks through your 2-10 Builder Warranty, and makes sure every concern is resolved or scheduled.

Timeframes are typical for a single-family home on a crawl space or slab. Basements, larger homes and weather can add time. We'll give you a schedule for your home before we start.

After closing

Covered for 10 years.

Every home comes with our builder warranty and ten years of structural protection through Quality Builders Warranty.
