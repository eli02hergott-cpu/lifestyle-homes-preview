Calhoun Place Townhomes

New two-story rental townhomes on Calhoun Road, about 4 miles from Lander University. Each home has its own entrance, a one-car garage and covered front and back porches.

Downstairs, the kitchen, breakfast room and living room open to each other under 9-foot ceilings; the three bedrooms and laundry are upstairs. Residents share a pickleball court, covered pavilion with grills, a playground and 2.7 acres of open space, and every resident gets a free 10×15 storage unit at Lake Greenwood Storage.

  • 3 bedrooms, up to 3.5 baths
  • Up to 1,448 heated sq ft
  • 1-car attached garage
  • Pickleball, pavilion & playground
  • Free 10×15 storage unit
  • Anticipated rent $2,100/mo
