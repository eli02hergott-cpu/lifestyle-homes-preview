About Calhoun Place

Two-story townhomes with three bedrooms.

Each home has its own entrance, a one-car garage and a covered back porch. Downstairs, the kitchen, breakfast room and living room are open to each other under 9-foot ceilings. The three bedrooms and the laundry are upstairs.

Calhoun Place sits at the corner of Calhoun Road and Thornblade Drive. The streets inside the community are private, with a pickleball court, covered pavilion and playground in the middle of the site. Built by Lifestyle Homes.
