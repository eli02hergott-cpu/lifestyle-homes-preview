Build on your lot

Your land, anywhere. Our craftsmanship.

Lakefront, wooded acreage, a lot in town, or family land. If you have the property, we'll build your custom home on it, with the same quality and fair price as in our own communities.

  1. 1

    Walk the property together

    We look at access, slope, utilities, septic or sewer, and any HOA or county rules, then tell you honestly what it will take to build there.

  2. 2

    Choose or create the plan

    Pick from our collection, modify one, or bring your own. We help site the home for the best views, light and drainage.

  3. 3

    Clear pricing up front

    You get a detailed price for your home on your land, including site work, before anything starts.

  4. 4

    We handle the rest

    Permits, clearing, foundation, construction, landscaping and your walkthrough, with an owner on the job every day.

Bring your own plan

Already have a design? We'll build it.

Whether you're working with an architect, found a plan you love online, or have a sketch on a napkin, we'll price it, refine it with you and build it.

  • Architect or online plans. We review them for your site, local code and budget, and flag anything before you commit.
  • Start from ours. Every plan in the Lifestyle Collection can be modified: move walls, add a bonus room, rework the primary suite.
  • Just an idea? We'll work with you and our designers to turn it into buildable plans.
  • Your finishes. Exterior style, materials, trim, cabinetry, fixtures, all chosen by you, or by us if you'd rather.
