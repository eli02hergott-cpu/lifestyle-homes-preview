The Plantation

Gated lakefront peninsula with an equestrian center

Another of Grand Harbor's sister communities, this exclusive gated peninsula offers breathtaking views and access to Lake Greenwood from large, private homesites, and is home to a lovingly cared-for equestrian center and riding trails. You can have it all: privacy, waterfront, horseback riding, and all the amenities of belonging to Grand Harbor Golf and Yacht Club.

Lifestyle Homes builds at The Plantation on select homesites. Ask us what's available.
