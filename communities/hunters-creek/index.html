Hunters Creek

Established neighborhood, large homesites

Lifestyle Homes, Greenwood County’s most renowned luxury builder, offers both custom and customized homes on a stunning array of homesites sprinkled within the grandeur of Hunters Creek.

This well-established, beautifully landscaped Greenwood community offers spacious homesites, beautifully landscaped areas and many community activities. Close to several of the areas private schools, Hunter’s Creek is a great choice for growing families.

Build your dream–your plan or one from the Lifestyle Collection on exceptionally large homesites that feature both open space and private wooded views. We understand you—and know that you yearn to build that perfect home–exactly what your family needs, wants and desires. We’ll help you find the right homesite, the plan that makes your dreams come true, the interior design options that thrill your heart and we’ll deliver your perfectly finished home within your budget and in a timeframe that will leave you awestruck.
