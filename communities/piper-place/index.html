Piper Place

Acreage homesites in Columbia County

Coming soon

Room to breathe in Columbia County, Georgia. Piper Place will offer 18 private homesites of 2.5 to 5 acres along Cobbham Road, minutes from Appling and a short drive to Evans and Augusta.

Quiet cul-de-sac streets, wooded and open homesites, and plenty of space for a pool, a garden or a big backyard, each with its own custom Lifestyle home. Every homesite has a private well and septic.
