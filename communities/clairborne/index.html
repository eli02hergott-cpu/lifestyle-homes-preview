Clairborne

Luxury 55+ paired townhomes

Clairborne, Lifestyle Home’s luxury 55+ neighborhood, is one of Greenwood’s newest communities with customized, paired townhomes designed for active adults offering a low-maintenance lifestyle.

The open floor plan design you’ve dreamed about—everything on the first floor with upstairs bonus options. Great room with fireplace, inviting kitchen island and breakfast room, wet bar options, screened porch—all perfect for easy living and entertaining. Huge primary suite and spa like ensuite. Of course your home will boast deep crown molding, granite, luxury flooring, tile, gas heat and appliances. This enclave is located minutes from uptown Greenwood and shopping districts. The clubhouse and pickleball courts are perfect backdrops for community activities and clubs. Best of all, Clairborne has partnered with the YMCA, located less than a minute away, offering group classes on balance and strength training, water aerobics, Pilates, and additional activities. This community is perfect for those looking for an active, luxurious, lifestyle that is convenient to town.
