Home/Warranty
Builder Warranty

Covering your home for 10 years.

Fast, reliable warranty repairs, well beyond what South Carolina requires.

We always strive for perfection, but we know warranties exist for a reason. Lifestyle Homes partners with Liberty Mutual's Quality Builders Warranty to cover your home for a full decade.

Year 1
Lifestyle Homes

Fit & finish

New homes in the South naturally settle as moisture leaves the framing, which can show up as small drywall or trim cracks. Keep a running list (an outlet that doesn't work, a gap in a crown joint) and call us at the 11-month mark. We'll do a first-year walkthrough and take care of the repairs.

Years 1–2
Lifestyle Homes

Structural items

We warrant all structural items for the first two years, in addition to the first-year coverage.

Years 3–10
Quality Builders Warranty

Major structural protection

For the following eight years, contact Quality Builders Warranty (a Liberty Mutual partner) directly for anything listed in your protection plan. Your home is registered at closing, and you'll receive a full warranty book and online portal access.

Transferable

The remainder of the 10-year warranty transfers to a new owner if you sell within those first ten years.

What's not covered

Landscaping, since weather puts shrub and sod health outside our control. Your contract's builder addendum lists the full limitations and what the Quality Builders Warranty program includes.

Need a repair?

Homeowners in their first two years can call us at 706-210-5488 or send a request.
