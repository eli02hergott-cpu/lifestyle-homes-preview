About Lifestyle Homes

Building homes & developing communities.

Where value, location and lifestyle come together, for every season of your life.

Our story

A quarter century of dream homes.

Lifestyle Homes is the evolution of Todd Bailey's original building company, R. T. Bailey, Inc., founded in 2000.

With a laser focus on custom homes, the business thrived on Todd's attention to detail and his patience in working with each customer to deliver a home that inspired pride in everyone involved.

Since then, it has grown to include many multi-million-dollar homes, mid-range luxury homes, entry-level single-family homes and townhomes, with the same commitment and quality in every one. Todd's motto: the home being bought is that buyer's dream home, whether it's $200,000 or $2,000,000.

Builder and developer

We develop the land, too.

In the mid-2000s Todd began developing land to build on, and buying land from other developers. Seeing a community from both the builder's and the buyer's side made for better homesites and a better finished product.

Since then, Todd has developed more than 15 communities, and there are usually three to five projects in different stages of engineering, development and building at any one time.

In 2024 we deepened our commitment to Greenwood County by purchasing the remaining golf course lots at Grand Harbor in Ninety Six. That lets us offer everything from attached paired homes to homesites of an acre or more, alongside lakefront living at The Retreat at Grand Harbor and new neighborhoods like Patriots Landing.

An owner on every job

Ownership you can see on site.

As the business grew, Lifestyle Homes, LLC was created to give key employees a stake in the company. With Rodney Scruggs as a partner, there is an owner on each job, every day, making sure nothing is sacrificed and every customer is satisfied.

With offices in Evans, Georgia and Greenwood, South Carolina, we help with every part of the process: homesite selection, plans, design selections, financing, construction and landscaping. And we've kept our custom-building roots, designing and building for owners of their own lakefront or wooded property.
